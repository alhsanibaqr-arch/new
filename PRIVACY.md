# Vardely Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Vardely is an on-device focus and app access tool.

## Information stored on your device

Vardely stores the package names of apps you select, whether app blocking is enabled, your accessibility disclosure choice, a salted hash of an optional PIN, temporary unlock times, and daily counts of blocked attempts. This information stays in the app's private storage on your device.

## Accessibility service

If you enable Android Accessibility for Vardely, the service checks the package name of the currently open app. It compares that name with the apps you selected to block or lock. It does not read screen text, capture passwords, record keystrokes, or collect the content inside other apps.

Vardely does not send this information to a server. This version has no account, advertising, analytics, or network feature.

## Your choices and data deletion

You can turn off Vardely's Accessibility service in Android Settings, turn off app blocking in Vardely, and remove app locks in Vardely. Uninstalling Vardely clears its local app data.

## Changes

This policy describes version 0.2.0. It must be updated if a later version adds online services, advertising, analytics, or new data practices.
